Gopuram ഗോപുരം

The gateway tower that marks the start of sacred ground: timber and laterite under tiers of sloping roofs, with a carved gable and a threshold you step over, never on.

Vilakku വിളക്ക്

The deepastambham, a tiered brass lamp tower, is lit at dusk. Every wick is a small offering to the deity.

Kathakali കഥകളി

Painted faces and towering crowns tell stories of gods and goddesses from the Ramayana, Mahabharata and Puranas, from dusk until dawn, lit by a single great lamp.

Afterlight

The temple does not close behind you. The pond, the gateway, and the sanctum are still there when the noise gets loud. Same path. Different lamps.
