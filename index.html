An offering to the gods: painted faces and towering crowns tell stories of gods and goddesses from the Ramayana, Mahabharata and Puranas, from dusk until dawn.

Kathakali കഥകളി

The gopuram is the gateway tower that marks the start of sacred ground. In Kerala it is built of timber and laterite under tiers of sloping copper-tiled roofs, with carved gables and a threshold you step over, never on.

Gopuram ഗോപുരം

The deepastambham, a tiered brass lamp tower, is lit at dusk. Every wick is a small offering to the deity.

Vilakku വിളക്ക്

Afterlight

The temple does not close behind you. The pond, the gateway, and the sanctum are still there when the noise gets loud. Same path. Different lamps.
